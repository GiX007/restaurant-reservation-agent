# Frequently asked questions

Source for the agent's INFORM action. Facts only, no rules with numbers —
deposits, minimum spend and cancellation deadlines live in `policy.json`.
If a question is not answered here, the agent escalates. It never guesses.

---

## Where are you and how do I get there?

Venue X is at [STREET], [AREA], [ISLAND] 84600, Greece.

Taxis and ride apps drop off at the main entrance. There is a free car park
across the road with about 30 spaces, unattended. Many guests arrive by
hotel transfer; we do not run our own shuttle.

## Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. No beachwear, no swimwear, no flip-flops in the evening.
The terrace gets windy after midnight, so bring a layer.

## Can I bring children?

Children are welcome for dinner until 22:00. After 22:00 the venue is
adults only, 18+, and staff may ask for ID.

## Is the venue accessible?

The main area and the accessible toilet are on one level, reachable by ramp
from the car park side. The bar is up two steps. Tell us when you book and
we will seat you accordingly.

## What payment methods do you accept?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash in euros.
Deposits are paid online through the payment link we send.

## Can I smoke?

Only on the outdoor terrace. Smoking is not allowed in the indoor areas.
Vaping follows the same rule.

## Do you have WiFi?

Yes, the network is "VenueX-Guest" and the password is printed on the table card.

## Can I take photos and videos?

Photos are fine. Professional cameras, tripods and drones need permission
in advance.

## I have a food allergy or a dietary requirement. Can you adapt a dish?

Tell us when you book and again when you order. The kitchen can leave out
or change many ingredients. For anything specific, a member of the team
will confirm with the kitchen before you order. We do not answer allergen
questions from memory.

## Can I bring a cake, or arrange something for a birthday?

Outside cakes are not allowed. We can arrange a dessert with a candle and
a sparkler. Tell us when you book.

## I left something behind. What do I do?

We keep lost property for 30 days. Write to lostandfound@venuex.example
with the date, the table and a description, and we will look.

## Are dogs allowed?

Assistance dogs are always welcome until 22:00. Other dogs are not allowed.

## What happens if the weather is bad?

We open in any weather. The main area is covered; if it is very windy the
terrace tables move inside, and the venue may look fuller than usual.

## Do you have parking for scooters and ATVs?

Yes, in the same car park, in the marked area near the entrance.
